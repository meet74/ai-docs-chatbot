Getting to Danforth and parking

Free parking is available behind the building, with entrance from Birch Alley. The clinic is a five minute walk from the subway and is served by two bus routes.

Lakeshore clinic

2200 Example Street, Unit 4, Toronto, ON M8V 0B2. Phone (416) 555-0178.

Lakeshore hours

  • Monday to Friday: 9:00 am to 5:00 pm
  • Saturday and Sunday: Closed

Getting to Lakeshore and parking

Street parking is available on Example Street, and there are six spaces in the plaza lot. A streetcar stops at the corner.

Holidays

Both clinics are closed on statutory holidays, including New Year's Day, Family Day, Good Friday, Victoria Day, Canada Day, the Civic Holiday, Labour Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas Day and Boxing Day. If your appointment falls on a holiday we will call you to reschedule.
