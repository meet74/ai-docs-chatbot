# New Patient FAQ

## Are you accepting new patients?

Yes. Both clinics are accepting new adult patients and families. We see patients of all ages.

## What happens at the first visit?

Your first visit takes about 75 minutes. It includes a comprehensive exam, x-rays if you need them, a review of your health history, and a talk with your dentist about a treatment plan. A cleaning is usually done at the same visit when there is time.

## What should I bring?

- Photo ID
- Your insurance card, or your Canada Dental Care Plan letter
- A list of your medications and allergies
- Recent x-rays, if you have them. We can also request them from your previous clinic with your written consent.

## Forms

Please complete the new patient forms online at least 24 hours before your visit. The link is in your confirmation email. If you cannot use a computer, come 20 minutes early and we will help you fill in the paper forms.

## Languages

Our team speaks English, French, Gujarati, Hindi and Mandarin. Ask us if you would like an interpreter for another language and we will try to arrange one.

## Nervous about the dentist?

Tell us when you book. We can plan a longer first visit, explain each step before we do it, and offer nitrous oxide for $60 per visit.

## Accessibility

Both clinics are wheelchair accessible, with step-free entrances and accessible washrooms.
