Maple Grove Dental Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. What we collect

We collect your name, address, phone number, email, date of birth, health history, insurance details, and the records of your care. We collect only what we need to look after you and to bill for your care.

2. How we use it

We use your information to provide dental care, to bill you and your insurer, and to send appointment reminders. We do not use your health information for marketing.

3. Who we share it with

We share information only with your insurer when we direct bill, with a specialist when you are referred, and with another clinic when you ask us to transfer your records. We do not sell your information to anyone, and we do not share it with advertisers.

4. How long we keep records

Adult records are kept for 10 years after your last visit. Records for children are kept until 10 years after the child's 18th birthday. After that we destroy them securely.

5. Online chat

The website chat assistant answers questions from our published documents. We do not store the content of chat conversations unless you ask to speak to a team member. In that case a transcript is sent to our staff so you do not have to repeat yourself, and it is deleted after 90 days. Please do not share health details or card numbers in the chat.

6. Access and corrections

You can ask to see your records or to correct them at any time. Send your request to privacy@maplegrovedental.example or ask at the front desk. We reply within 30 days.

7. Text and email reminders

You can opt out of reminders at any time by replying STOP to a text message or by using the link in an email. Opting out does not affect your care.

8. Questions and complaints

Our privacy officer can be reached at privacy@maplegrovedental.example. If you are not satisfied with our reply, you can contact the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Ontario.
