# Services and Prices

Maple Grove Dental offers general, preventive and restorative dentistry for adults at both of our Toronto clinics. All prices are in Canadian dollars, before tax and before any insurance coverage. Children's visits are priced separately, see the Children and Teens page.

## Exams and cleanings

- Comprehensive new patient exam with x-rays: $145
- Routine recall exam: $65
- Standard cleaning (scale and polish, about 30 minutes): $120
- Extended cleaning (about 60 minutes, for patients with gum disease or heavy build-up): $195
- Fluoride treatment: $35

## X-rays

- Bitewing x-rays (two to four images): $45
- Panoramic x-ray: $95

Digital x-rays use a low radiation dose. We will not take x-rays that your dentist does not need, and we can request recent images from your previous clinic.

## Fillings and extractions

| Treatment | Price |
| --- | --- |
| White filling, one surface | $210 |
| White filling, two surfaces | $265 |
| White filling, three or more surfaces | $320 |
| Simple extraction | $230 |
| Surgical extraction | $420 |

Our fillings are tooth-coloured composite. We do not place silver amalgam fillings.

## Root canal treatment

Root canal treatment is priced by tooth position:

- Front tooth: $890
- Premolar: $1,050
- Molar: $1,290

A crown is usually needed after a root canal on a molar. The permanent crown is priced separately below.

## Crowns and night guards

- Porcelain crown: $1,350
- Custom night guard for teeth grinding: $450

## Whitening

- In-office whitening (one visit, about 90 minutes): $480
- Take-home whitening trays with gel: $320

Whitening is not recommended while you have untreated cavities or gum disease. Your dentist will check this at your exam.

## Clear aligners

The aligner consultation is free. Treatment starts from $4,800 depending on how many trays you need. Your dentist will give you a written quote after scans are taken.

## Sedation

Nitrous oxide (laughing gas) is available for anxious patients at $60 per visit.

## Emergency visits

An emergency exam is $135. Any treatment needed on the day is charged in addition, at the prices on this page.
