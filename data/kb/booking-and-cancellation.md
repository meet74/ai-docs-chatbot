# Booking and Cancellation Policy

## How to book

You can book online at maplegrovedental.example/book, by phone at (416) 555-0142 (Danforth) or (416) 555-0178 (Lakeshore), or at the front desk. Online booking is open 24 hours a day. Routine cleanings are often booked two to three weeks ahead.

## Reminders

We send a text message reminder 48 hours before your appointment and another 24 hours before it. Reply C to confirm. Reply STOP to turn text reminders off.

## Changing or cancelling

Please give us at least 24 hours notice if you need to cancel or reschedule. You can do this by phone, by replying to the reminder text, or online. Notice makes the time available for someone who is waiting in pain.

## Late cancellation and no-show fees

- Cancelling with less than 24 hours notice, or missing an appointment: $60 for appointments up to 60 minutes
- The same, for appointments longer than 60 minutes: $100

The fee is not covered by insurance. We waive the fee the first time it happens. We also waive it if you cancel because of illness or an emergency and tell us as soon as you can.

## Arriving late

Please arrive 10 minutes early for your first visit and 5 minutes early after that. If you are more than 15 minutes late, we may need to shorten the visit or reschedule it, and the late cancellation fee can apply.

## Cancellation list

If you would like an earlier appointment, ask to join the cancellation list. We call or text you when a slot opens up on the same day.
