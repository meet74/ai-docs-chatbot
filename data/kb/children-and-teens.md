# Children and Teens

## First visit

We recommend a first dental visit by a child's first birthday, or within six months after the first tooth appears. The first visit is short and relaxed. Parents are welcome to stay in the room for every visit.

## Prices for patients under 12

- Exam and cleaning: $95
- Fluoride varnish: $35
- Sealant, per tooth: $45
- Custom sports mouthguard: $120

Patients aged 12 to 17 are charged the adult prices on the Services and Prices page, except for the custom mouthguard.

## Appointment times

After-school appointments are available at the Danforth clinic on Tuesdays and Thursdays until 7 pm. Please book these early because they fill fast.

## Baby teeth

Baby teeth hold space for adult teeth, so cavities in them are treated. Start brushing as soon as the first tooth appears, with a smear of fluoride toothpaste the size of a grain of rice. From age three, use a pea-sized amount.

## Sports and braces

Children who play contact sports should wear a custom mouthguard. We can fit one in a single visit. We refer children who need orthodontic assessment to a specialist and share records with them.
