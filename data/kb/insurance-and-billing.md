# Insurance and Billing

## Direct billing

We direct bill most major Canadian group insurance plans. We send claims electronically on the day of your visit, so you usually only pay the portion your plan does not cover. Please bring your insurance card to every appointment, and tell us if your plan changes.

If we cannot direct bill your insurer, we give you a detailed receipt and you can submit the claim yourself. Insurers usually pay you back in one to two weeks.

## Canada Dental Care Plan

We are a registered provider for the Canada Dental Care Plan. Bring your approval letter or your card on your first visit so we can check what is covered.

## Treatment estimates and pre-approval

For work over $500 we send a written treatment estimate to your insurer. This is called pre-approval. Insurers normally reply within 10 business days. We will not start the work before you have seen the estimate, and you are free to ask for a second opinion.

## Payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, debit cards and Interac e-Transfer. We do not accept cheques or cash payments over $500. Your share of the bill is due at the end of each visit.

## Payment plans

For treatment over $500 you can split the cost into three equal monthly instalments with no interest, through Maple Pay. The first instalment is charged on the day of treatment. Ask the front desk to set this up before your visit.

## Receipts and tax

We email a receipt after every visit. You can use these receipts for the medical expense tax credit. Annual statements are available from the front desk on request.

## Overdue accounts

Invoices that are not paid within 30 days are charged a $15 administration fee. If you are having trouble paying, call the billing office on (416) 555-0142 before the due date and we will work something out.
